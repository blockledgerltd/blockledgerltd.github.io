[ FEATURES ]

Hybrid Consensus
First coin to fuse Scrypt PoW with PoS. Instant near-zero cost payments. Energy efficient by design. No single point of failure.
Private Transactions
Addresses starting with '5' are private. No mixing services. No master nodes. Just baked-in privacy on a decentralized P2P network.
Unique Emission
Lower block rewards than BTC. Designed scarcity. Stock-to-flow engineered for long-term value. No inflationary bullshit.
